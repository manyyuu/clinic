SMILE FUN FACTS

Your smile has a few surprises.

A little dental trivia, plus simple reminders that can make oral care easier to understand.

01Enamel is the hard stuff.

Tooth enamel is the hardest tissue in the human body, but it still deserves daily care.

02Teeth aren't just for smiling.

They help with biting, chewing and speaking — all reasons oral health is part of everyday wellbeing.

03Your gums matter too.

A healthy smile is about more than the visible teeth. Gums and the surrounding tissues deserve attention as well.

04Small habits add up.

Consistent brushing, cleaning between teeth and regular professional advice can all support oral health.

SMILE CHECK-IN

Three tiny questions for your next visit.

You don't need a diagnosis. Start with what you have noticed.

01Has anything changed?Sensitivity, discomfort, appearance or routine.
02What are you hoping to improve?Comfort, function, confidence or prevention.
03What are you unsure about?Bring the question — that's enough to start.

SMILE CARE, SIMPLIFIED

A few things your smile may be telling you.

01

Sensitivity

Repeated sensitivity to hot, cold or sweet foods is something worth mentioning rather than simply working around.

02

Bleeding gums

Bleeding during brushing or cleaning between teeth deserves attention and a conversation with a dental professional.

03

A changing bite

If the way your teeth meet feels different, tell your dentist. Small observations can help guide an assessment.
