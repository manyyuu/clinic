DIABETES & HEALTH FUN FACTS

A few things that make the big picture clearer.

Friendly facts for curiosity and conversation — not a replacement for personalised medical advice.

01Insulin is a messenger.

Insulin helps the body move glucose from the bloodstream into cells where it can be used for energy.

02Patterns can matter.

Keeping the measurements your clinician recommends can make trends easier to discuss than one isolated number.

03“Normal” is personal.

Your usual routine, medicines, meals and activity all provide context when you talk about changes.

04Small questions count.

You can bring up changes in thirst, energy, appetite or other observations even when you're not sure they matter.
